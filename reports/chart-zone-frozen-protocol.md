# Frozen chart-zone experiment and 2020 evaluation protocol

Frozen before retrieving or evaluating 2020. This is an experimental visual research feature, not a live trading system. It is off by default. It uses the same `vwmaCrossoverScript` candidate generation and scoring for chart and research extraction. The training data is only 2021–2025; 2025 was used for development threshold selection and is not a holdout. The separate earlier 20-feature offline model is not wired to the chart: this is a new, smaller chart-computable model fitted from the same years.

- Model JSON SHA-256: `e802cb90d19285b4a863b3c04c1cc34203261622ac3b4088a8c862c29ae0edad`.
- Defaults: exact chart crossover length 14, separation 0.15, M15 only; at least 220 loaded bars; signal candle must be completed and recent bars contiguous. Fixed 100-pip favorable/adverse movement, $0.01 per gold pip. Research zones are +/- 0.2 indicator ATR around an already confirmed swing or previous UTC-day extreme that the signal candle touched. They are not full course order blocks, volume profile, FVG or BOS/MSS zones. On chart, loaded history affects warmup; there is no guarantee that the loaded history is long enough to reproduce a separately downloaded sample.
- Fixed candidate decision windows: London 07:00–10:00 UTC, overlap 12:00–15:00 UTC, NY 15:00–17:00 UTC; cut off by 12:45 America/New_York, which can truncate the NY window. This is a conservative historical assumption, not a complete broker/holiday schedule or the entire NY session. No trades held across daily cutoff or missing intervals.
- Frozen score: logistic classifier, threshold >=0.20, trained on 2021–2025 chart-computable features, with same-bar target/stop ambiguity treated as adverse for training. Threshold selected from 0.10 through 0.50 at 0.05 steps based on best 2025 development average among thresholds with at least 30 resolved observations; refit on all 2021–2025 afterward. The selection was negative even on 2025: 8 wins of 75 resolved and approximately -$71.75/oz aggregated under the illustrative fixed-hit assumptions. It did not establish an edge; the score is not a calibrated probability of profit.
- 2020 test: extract candidates using the frozen chart script itself. Compare all candidates with score-selected suggestions, by direction and session. Enter hypothetically at the next contiguous open (long ask + $0.10/oz, short bid - $0.10/oz); evaluate opposite-side bid/ask candle OHLC against +/-$1 levels, stop on ambiguous bars for conservative fixed-hit outcome, and subtract $0.10/oz exit slippage plus illustrative $0.07/oz commission. Report unresolved/gap/cutoff cases separately, not as losses or zero-cost trades. Report adverse-first later recovery as counterfactual price behavior, not profit after a stop. Events may overlap and are not a sequential account or live fills.
- 2020 is temporally earlier than the training years: it is untouched by this model's development but is a backward cross-regime check, not a prospective forward test. No further tuning using 2020 should be described as unseen evaluation.
